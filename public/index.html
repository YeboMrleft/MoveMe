Got questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to use Move-Me?

Posting a job is free. A small R10 platform fee applies when you accept a driver's offer. The driver sets their own price — you compare and choose the best deal.

How are drivers verified?

Every driver submits their South African ID, driver's licence, vehicle licence disc, number plate photo, and 4-angle bakkie photos. Our team manually reviews and approves each driver before they can go online.

Do I need to verify my identity as a customer?

Yes. To protect both drivers and the community, customers are required to verify their identity with a selfie and SA ID number before posting their first job. Your information is stored securely and only accessed in the event of a dispute or reported incident.

What areas does Move-Me operate in?

Move-Me operates across South Africa — including Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban, Pretoria, Port Elizabeth, Bloemfontein, and Pietermaritzburg. We're growing fast, so your city may be added soon.

What can I move?

Almost anything that fits in a bakkie! Furniture, appliances, building materials, boxes, marketplace purchases, student belongings, garden waste, and small business deliveries. You can also add a photo of your load when posting.

How do I pay?

Payments are handled securely in the app via PayFast — South Africa's leading payment gateway. You can pay by card. No cash, no hassle.

What if something goes wrong during my move?

All users — both senders and drivers — are identity-verified. In the event of a dispute, you can report the issue directly in the app and our team will investigate. Because all users are vetted, there is a full audit trail.

How quickly will I get a driver?

Most jobs receive offers within minutes. Driver availability depends on your area and time of day. Scheduling a job in advance guarantees a driver is ready when you need them.

Can I schedule a move in advance?

Yes — when posting a job you can select "Schedule for later" and choose your preferred date and time. Your job will be posted to drivers in advance.

How does the driver rating system work?

After every completed trip, both the sender and driver can rate each other. Ratings build trust and reputation. Top-rated drivers appear on the leaderboard and earn priority visibility to new jobs.
